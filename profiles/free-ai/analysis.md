# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how valuable this item is for a reader hunting FREE AI capabilities: free APIs, free tiers, free compute, and practical ways to use them. Anything without a concrete free-access path is NOT valuable here, no matter how important it is otherwise.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Jackpot.** New free API or free tier with strong models, free GPU/compute programs, major quota boosts, or a paid tool going free.
- **7-8: High value.** Useful free tools or APIs for AI agents, open models with free hosted access, significant free-tier improvements, price-to-free changes, practical guides built on free tools.
- **5-6: Minor.** Small freebies, weak-model free tiers, incremental free-quota changes, rumors of upcoming free offerings worth watching.
- **3-4: Off-focus.** Paid launches, general AI news with no free angle, routine updates, tutorials requiring paid tools.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, purely promotional content, or anything with no connection to free AI.

# Evaluation guidance

The ONLY question that matters: does this give a concrete free capability or a practical free path? Score 7+ requires a clear free-access route (signup conditions, card requirements, quotas). Treat vendor claims skeptically: "free" with a card wall or a tiny trial scores 5 at best. Do not reward famous names or hype without free substance. Use three to five specific topic tags.

# Kitchen intel

When the item is a comparison, ranking, awesome-list, or hub of free AI options, extract the TOP 3-5 concrete free offers as a compact list inside `summary`: provider — model — exact quota — card? (yes/no). Skip offers you can tell are dead or card-walled; say so in one clause when the source itself is outdated.
