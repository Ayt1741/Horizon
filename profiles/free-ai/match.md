# Free AI hunt profile

Use this profile ONLY for news about FREE AI capabilities and free tools that empower an AI agent: free LLM/chat APIs and free tiers, free GPU or compute quotas, free search/scraping/dev APIs, open models with free hosted access, price drops to free, quota boosts, and practical guides for free AI tools.

Typical items include new free API tiers, free-model releases with hosted access, free GPU programs, free-tier changes, comparisons of free options, and tutorials built on free tools.

Do NOT use this profile for paid-only launches, general tech news without a free-access path, hardware, finance, crypto, politics, or promotional content without a substantive free offering.

HUNT PRIORITY (score these highest): no-signup/keyless LLM APIs; new accounts with free API credit and NO credit card; quota boosts and rate-limit raises on free tiers; strong open models (70B+ class or notably efficient) gaining free hosted/API access; new free inference providers and gateways; verified comparisons/datasets of free LLM APIs ("kitchens": awesome-lists, free-API hubs, daily rankings); geo-block lifts and new regions for free tiers; price-to-free changes; free GPU programs (Colab/Kaggle-style quotas, hackathons, research credits).

STRENGTH BAR: a free tier serving weak models only is minor news (5-6 at best); free access to strong/flagship-class models is the jackpot. Always capture the HOW: signup steps, card? (yes/no), exact quotas (RPM/RPD/TPM), context window, OpenAI-compatibility, geo-blocks.
