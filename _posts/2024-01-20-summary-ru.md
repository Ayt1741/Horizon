## Discrete and continuous mathematical models of sharp-fronted collective cell migration and invasion

_arXiv · 2024-01-20 · score 0.8_

Mathematical models describing the spatial spreading and invasion of populations of biological cells are often developed in a continuum modelling framework using reaction-diffusion equations. While continuum models based on linear diffusion are routinely employed and known to capture key experimental observations, linear diffusion fails to predict well-defined sharp fronts that are often observed experimentally.
