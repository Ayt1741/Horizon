## Fully nonlinear second-order mean field games with nondifferentiable Hamiltonians

_arXiv · 2025-11-16 · score 0.8_

We analyse fully nonlinear second-order mean field games (MFG) with nondifferentiable Hamiltonians, which take the form of a coupled system of a fully nonlinear Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation and a Kolmogorov-Fokker-Planck partial differential inclusion (PDI) featuring the set-valued subdifferential of the Hamiltonian. We show the existence of solutions of some stationary MFG systems with quite general coupling operators and nonnegative distributional source terms, on general bounded convex domains, under the primary assumptions of uniform ellipticity and the Cordes condition on the diffusion coefficient.
