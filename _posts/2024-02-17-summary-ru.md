## Towards Development of Automated Knowledge Maps and Databases for Materials Engineering using Large Language Models

_arXiv · 2024-02-17 · score 0.8_

In this work a Large Language Model (LLM) based workflow is presented that utilizes OpenAI ChatGPT model GPT-3.5-turbo-1106 and Google Gemini Pro model to create summary of text, data and images from research articles. It is demonstrated that by using a series of processing, the key information can be arranged in tabular form and knowledge graphs to capture underlying concepts.
