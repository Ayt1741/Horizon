## Keypoint Action Tokens Enable In-Context Imitation Learning in Robotics

_arXiv · 2024-10-17 · score 0.825_

We achieve this by transforming visual observations (inputs) and trajectories of actions (outputs) into sequences of tokens that a text-pretrained Transformer (GPT-4 Turbo) can ingest and generate, via a framework we call Keypoint Action Tokens (KAT).
