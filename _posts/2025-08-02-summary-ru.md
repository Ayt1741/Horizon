## ELFATT: Efficient Linear Fast Attention for Vision Transformers

_arXiv · 2025-08-02 · score 0.825_

The attention mechanism is the key to the success of transformers in different machine learning tasks. However, the quadratic complexity with respect to the sequence length of the vanilla softmax-based attention mechanism becomes the major bottleneck for the application of long sequence tasks, such as vision tasks.
