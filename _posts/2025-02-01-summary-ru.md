## IsolateGPT: An Execution Isolation Architecture for LLM-Based Agentic Systems

_arXiv · 2025-02-01 · score 0.825_

Large language models (LLMs) extended as systems, such as ChatGPT, have begun supporting third-party applications. These LLM apps leverage the de facto natural language-based automated execution paradigm of LLMs: that is, apps and their interactions are defined in natural language, provided access to user data, and allowed to freely interact with each other and the system.
