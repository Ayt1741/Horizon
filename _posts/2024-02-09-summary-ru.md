## Wasserstein proximal operators describe score-based generative models and resolve memorization

_arXiv · 2024-02-09 · score 0.8_

We first formulate SGMs in terms of the Wasserstein proximal operator (WPO) and demonstrate that, via mean-field games (MFGs), the WPO formulation reveals mathematical structure that describes the inductive bias of diffusion and score-based models.
