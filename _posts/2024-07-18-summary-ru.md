## MinD-3D: Reconstruct High-quality 3D objects in Human Brain

_arXiv · 2024-07-18 · score 0.8_

## On Pretraining Data Diversity for Self-Supervised Learning

_arXiv · 2024-07-18 · score 0.8_

Our findings consistently demonstrate that increasing pretraining data diversity enhances SSL performance, albeit only when the distribution distance to the downstream data is minimal.
